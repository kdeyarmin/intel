## Slide 3: What is CareMetric Intel?

A platform that turns public Medicare/CMS datasets and the NPPES registry into actionable intelligence for healthcare sales, business development, and market research.

**It helps you:**
- Find and understand providers & facilities
- Build ranked lead lists and run outreach
- Analyze referral networks, markets, counties, and territories
- Use AI to summarize, enrich, and surface insights

Built for multi-organization use with roles, plans, and data isolation.

---

## Slide 4: Why an Employee Pilot?

- Validate real-world usefulness before external customers
- Confirm data accuracy and freshness on live CMS/NPPES loads
- Identify friction in key workflows
- Stress-test operational reliability (imports, AI, monitoring)
- Collect candid feedback from people who know the domain

**Goal:** Decide whether to expand based on evidence, not assumptions.

## Slide 6: The Six Pilot Workflows

1. Provider / Facility Search → Detail pages  
   *Does the CMS data feel accurate and useful for targeting?*

2. Lead list building + scoring + outreach/campaigns  
   *Can you go from discovery to action efficiently?*

3. Data Center + Import Monitor  
   *Is data freshness visible and trustworthy?*

4. AI Assistant + enrichment bots  
   *Quality, usefulness, and any surprising limits?*

5. Territory / Market / County views  
   *Do the analytics respect your plan and make sense?*

6. Multi-user roles, data isolation & billing  
   *Does access feel correct and secure?*

---

## Slide 7: How the Pilot Works

- **Duration:** 1–2 weeks
- **Participants:** 5–10 employees
- **Access:** Accounts created / approved by admin; roles assigned
- **Support:** In-app Help (`/Help`), Import Monitor Health tab, designated feedback channel
- **Expectations:** Use the product for real or realistic tasks; note what works, what confuses, and what’s missing
- **Ops behind the scenes:** Maintenance ticks keep data fresh; we monitor Health tab and alerts

## Slide 8: What We Need From You

Please focus feedback on:

- **Usefulness** — Does the intelligence help you target or prioritize?
- **Accuracy** — Does CMS / NPPES data look complete and correct for your use case?
- **Friction** — Where did you get stuck or lose time?
- **Clarity** — Are scores, market share, territory limits, and empty states understandable?
- **Performance** — Any slow pages, timeouts, or surprising AI costs/limits?
- **Gaps** — Missing features or data that would make this much more valuable?

Capture screenshots or specific examples when possible.

---

## Slide 9: Monitoring & Reliability

We will watch:

- Import Monitor → Health tab (last tick, “Needs attention”)
- Operations alert emails (stale datasets, failed schedules, old rollups)
- Server logs and AI/email quota usage

You should also glance at the Health tab if something looks off.

**Note:** Data freshness depends on maintenance ticks. If the Health tab shows an overdue heartbeat, tell us.

---

## Slide 10: Success Criteria (Go / No-Go)

We’ll expand beyond the pilot if:

- Multiple users successfully complete the core workflows
- No critical isolation or permission issues appear
- Data freshness stays healthy for several days
- Feedback indicates the product is useful enough to continue
- Operational issues are understood and manageable

Otherwise we address specific gaps first.
