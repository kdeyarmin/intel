## Slide 3: What is CareMetric?

**Public data → Private advantage**

- Search & understand providers + facilities (CMS KPIs, referrals, affiliations)
- Build ranked lead lists and run multi-channel outreach
- Analyze referral networks, markets, counties, and territories
- AI Assistant + enrichment bots that summarize and fill gaps

Multi-tenant, role-based, plan-scoped. Built for healthcare sales, BD, and market teams.

**Speaker script (~90 sec):**  
"At its core, CareMetric takes the massive public Medicare and NPPES datasets and turns them into something you can actually use day-to-day. Instead of downloading giant CSVs or bouncing between CMS sites, you search, you open a detail page that already has the KPIs, referral signals, and linked facilities, and you can drop people straight into a lead list. There’s an AI layer that can summarize a provider or help you find emails. And everything is scoped to your organization and plan so you only see what you’re entitled to. The question we need you to answer is: does this actually save you time and help you prioritize better?"

## Slide 4: Why an Employee Pilot **Now**?

- Validate usefulness before any external customers
- Confirm CMS / NPPES accuracy and freshness under real loads
- Find the friction before it costs us customers
- Stress-test imports, AI limits, and monitoring
- Get domain-expert feedback while we can still change direction cheaply

**Goal:** Expand only if the evidence says we should.

**Speaker script (~60 sec):**  
"We could have just opened it up. Instead we’re doing this pilot first because the cost of being wrong is high. If the data feels incomplete, if the lead-list flow is clunky, or if the AI is more annoying than helpful, we want to know while it’s still just us. Your job is to be the canary. If something doesn’t make sense to you, it won’t make sense to a customer."

## Slide 6: Workflows 1–3 — Deep Dive

**1. Search → Detail**  
Search by name, NPI, specialty, or location. Open the provider or facility. Look at KPIs, CMS clinical data, referrals, linked facilities, AI summary.  
*Ask yourself: Would I use this instead of my current process?*

**2. Lead Lists + Outreach**  
Build a list, review scores, create (or preview) a campaign. Check the CAN-SPAM footer.  
*Ask yourself: How many clicks from “interesting provider” to “ready to contact”?*

**3. Data Center + Import Monitor**  
Open the Health tab. Look at last tick, “Needs attention,” and dataset freshness.  
*Ask yourself: Do I trust that the data is current?*

**Speaker script (~2 min):**  
"Let’s make these concrete. For search and detail, I want you to pick a real provider or facility you care about and see if the page tells you something you didn’t already know. For lead lists, try to build a short list and get it ready for outreach — count the clicks and note where you hesitated. For the Import Monitor, just open the Health tab once a day and tell us if the ‘Needs attention’ list makes sense or looks scary. Screenshots of anything confusing are gold."

---

## Slide 7: Workflows 4–6 — Deep Dive

**4. AI Assistant + Bots**  
Ask the Assistant about a market or provider. Trigger an email search or quality scan on a small set.  
*Ask yourself: Did it save time or just create work?*

**5. Territory / Market / County**  
Open the views under your plan. Check that results stay inside your territory and that the explanations make sense.  
*Ask yourself: Would this change how I prioritize a county?*

**6. Roles & Isolation**  
Log in as different roles if possible. Confirm you cannot see another org’s campaigns or lists.  
*Ask yourself: Does access feel safe?*

**Speaker script (~2 min):**  
"AI is the wild card. Try the Assistant for a real question you’d normally research manually, then tell us if the answer was worth the wait. The enrichment bots are great for spotting missing emails or data-quality issues — run them on a tiny set so we don’t burn quota. Territory and market views should respect whatever plan we put you on; if you see data you shouldn’t, that’s a bug. Finally, if you have a chance to switch roles, try to peek at another organization’s stuff — you should get a clean 404. That isolation is non-negotiable."

## Slide 9: What We Need From You (the good stuff)

Please focus on:

- **Usefulness** — Did this help you target or prioritize?
- **Accuracy** — Did the CMS / NPPES data look right for your use case?
- **Friction** — Where did you get stuck or lose time?
- **Clarity** — Were scores, market share, and empty states understandable?
- **Surprises** — AI limits, slow pages, missing data, anything weird

**Bonus:** One thing you loved and one thing that annoyed you.

**Speaker script (~75 sec):**  
"Here’s the feedback that actually changes the product. Tell us if it was useful, if the data felt accurate, and where you got stuck. The more specific the better — ‘the referral partners card on provider detail was missing X’ is way more valuable than ‘it was okay.’ And I really want the one thing you loved and the one thing that annoyed you. Those two sentences often point us at the highest-leverage fixes."
