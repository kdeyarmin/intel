## Slide 3: What is CareMetric Intel?

A healthcare provider intelligence, CRM, and analytics platform.

It ingests public Medicare/CMS datasets and the NPPES registry, then surfaces:

- Provider & facility profiles with CMS quality, utilization, and financial data
- Referral networks and market insights
- Lead lists with scoring
- Multi-channel outreach and campaigns
- AI-powered summaries and enrichment
- Territory, county, and market views

**Purpose:** Give sales and business-development teams better targeting and execution tools.

---

## Slide 4: Why an Employee Pilot?

The product is feature-complete and has been thoroughly reviewed.

We need real users to tell us:

- Is the intelligence useful and accurate?
- Do the workflows feel natural?
- What’s missing or confusing?
- How does it perform with everyday use?

Your feedback will decide what we fix before opening it to external customers.

---

## Slide 5: What You Can Do

- Search providers and facilities by name, NPI, specialty, or location
- View rich detail pages with CMS KPIs, quality ratings, and linked intelligence
- Build scored lead lists and run outreach
- Explore referral networks and market/territory data
- Use the AI Assistant for quick insights
- Monitor data freshness (Import Monitor Health tab)

Everything is role-aware and organization-scoped.

---

## Slide 6: Six Workflows to Try

Please walk through these end-to-end and note anything that feels off:

1. **Search → Provider / Facility Detail** — Is the CMS data accurate and useful?
2. **Lead Lists, Scoring & Outreach** — Can you build a list and start a campaign?
3. **Data Center & Import Monitor** — Can you see freshness and health?
4. **AI Assistant & Enrichment** — Are the answers helpful?
5. **Territory / Market / County Views** — Do the limits and insights make sense?
6. **Roles & Data Isolation** — Can you only see what you should?

## Slide 7: How to Get Started

1. You will receive login credentials or an invite.
2. Log in and change your password if needed.
3. Open the in-app Help page (`/Help`) for a quick guide.
4. Start with a simple provider search.
5. Check the Import Monitor → Health tab to see data freshness.
6. Use the feedback channel we share (Slack / doc / email).

**Support:** In-app Help + your pilot point of contact.

## Slide 8: What Feedback We Need

Please share:

- **Usefulness** — Did the data help you understand a provider or market?
- **Accuracy** — Did CMS numbers and scores match your expectations?
- **Ease of use** — Where did you get stuck or confused?
- **Missing pieces** — What would make this more valuable?
- **Performance** — Any slow pages or unexpected errors?
- **Edge cases** — Empty states, permission issues, incomplete records.

Screenshots and specific NPIs/facilities are especially helpful.

## Slide 9: Timeline & Success Criteria

**Suggested duration:** 1–2 weeks of active use.

**We consider the pilot successful if:**

- Multiple people complete the six workflows
- No critical data-isolation or permission bugs appear
- Data freshness stays healthy
- Feedback shows the product is useful enough to continue

After the pilot we will decide whether to expand access or address specific gaps first.
