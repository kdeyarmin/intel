## Slide 3: What is CareMetric?

A healthcare provider intelligence + CRM + analytics platform.

**It turns public data into action:**
- Provider & facility profiles with CMS quality, utilization & financial data
- Referral networks and market insights
- Scored lead lists and multi-channel outreach
- AI summaries and enrichment
- Territory, county, and market views

**In one sentence:** Better targeting and execution for our sales and business-development teams.

**Speaker Script (~1.5 min):**  
“Imagine you need to find the right physicians or facilities in a specific market, understand their Medicare volume and quality scores, see who they refer to, and then reach out with a relevant message — all without bouncing between a dozen spreadsheets and government websites. That’s CareMetric. It pulls in the official CMS datasets and the NPPES registry, cleans and organizes them, and gives you searchable profiles, scores, networks, and tools to act. The goal is simple: spend less time hunting for data and more time having the right conversations.”

## Slide 7: Workflows 1–3 (Deep Dive)

**1. Search → Provider / Facility Detail**  
Search by name, NPI, specialty, or location → open a detail page → review KPIs, CMS data, locations, network, and linked intelligence.  
*Does the data feel accurate and useful for targeting?*

**2. Lead Lists, Scoring & Outreach**  
Build a list from search results → review scores and breakdown → create or preview a campaign.  
*Can you go from “interesting provider” to “outreach started” without friction?*

**3. Data Center & Import Monitor**  
Visit Data Center and Import Monitor → check the Health tab and “Needs attention” list.  
*Is data freshness clear and trustworthy?*

**Speaker Script (~2 min):**  
“Start with search. Pick a provider or facility you know and see if the page matches what you expect. Then try building a small lead list and see how the scoring feels. Finally, go to the Import Monitor and look at the Health tab — that’s how we know the data is current. If any of these steps feel confusing or the numbers look wrong, that’s exactly the feedback we need.”

---

## Slide 8: Workflows 4–6 (Deep Dive)

**4. AI Assistant & Enrichment**  
Ask the AI Assistant about a provider, market, or referral pattern. Try an enrichment or email search on a small set.  
*Are the answers useful? Any rate-limit surprises?*

**5. Territory / Market / County Views**  
Open Territory, County, and Market pages.  
*Do the insights make sense under your organization’s plan limits?*

**6. Roles & Data Isolation**  
Log in with different roles if possible. Confirm you cannot see another organization’s lists or campaigns.  
*Does the permission model feel correct and safe?*

**Speaker Script (~2 min):**  
“Next, try the AI Assistant — ask it something you would normally research yourself. Then explore the territory and market views and notice how they respect your organization’s plan. Finally, if you have access to more than one role, verify that data isolation works the way you expect. These last three are about trust: does the AI help, do the market views make sense, and can you only see what you should?”

## Slide 9: How to Get Started

3. Open the in-app Help page (`/Help`) for a quick guide.

## Slide 10: What Feedback We Need Most

Please share:

- **Usefulness** — Did the data help you understand a provider or market?
- **Accuracy** — Did CMS numbers and scores match your expectations?
- **Ease of use** — Where did you get stuck or confused?
- **Missing pieces** — What would make this more valuable?
- **Performance** — Any slow pages or unexpected errors?
- **Edge cases** — Empty states, permission issues, incomplete records.

Screenshots and specific NPIs or facilities are gold.

**Speaker Script (~1.5 min):**  
“The most valuable feedback is specific. Instead of ‘it was okay,’ tell us ‘the quality rating on this facility didn’t match what I know’ or ‘I couldn’t figure out how to add someone to a list.’ Screenshots and the actual NPI or facility name make it much easier for us to reproduce and fix things. Even small friction points matter.”

## Slide 11: Timeline & Success Criteria

**Suggested duration:** 1–2 weeks of active use.

**We consider the pilot successful if:**

- Multiple people complete the six workflows
- No critical data-isolation or permission bugs appear
- Data freshness stays healthy
- Feedback shows the product is useful enough to continue

After the pilot we will decide whether to expand access or address specific gaps first.

**Speaker Script (~1 min):**  
“Plan on using it for one to two weeks. We’ll consider the pilot a success if several of you complete the six workflows, we don’t find any critical isolation issues, the data stays fresh, and the overall feedback says this is worth continuing. Then we’ll decide together whether to open it more widely or fix the biggest gaps first.”
